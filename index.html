You’re ready for your first number

SafeSpends protects bills and minimum debt payments due before payday, protects your savings set-aside, and divides what remains across the days left.

The simple maths:
Balance − bills before payday − debt minimums before payday − savings set-aside = your Safe-to-Spend pool.

Payments due exactly on payday are not subtracted in this period, because the new income arrives that day.

The plan

One debt at a time

Two honest ways to aim extra money. Snowball clears the smallest balance first — quick wins, real momentum. Avalanche clears the highest interest first — the least total interest. Neither is “correct”; the best plan is the one you will stay with. Your minimums stay protected inside Safe-to-Spend either way.

Change it and watch the date below move. This is a picture, not a promise — interest is estimated from the APRs you add to each debt.

Envelopes

Add an envelope

Caps, not cages. An envelope is a monthly line to notice for one kind of spending. Going over is information, not failure — and envelopes never change your Safe-to-Spend number; the money was already counted once.

Milestones

Proof it is working

Kept, not chased. These are not points, and there is nothing to win. They are a record of what you have already done — kept for the days when it feels like nothing is working. Every one of them comes from your real numbers, never from a timer or a trick.
